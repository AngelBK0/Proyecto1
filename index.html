Future Jobs

People are going to work with robots and artificial intelligence. Some jobs, like drivers or cashiers, may not exist because machines will do them. But new jobs will appear in areas like technology and science. People are going to need to learn new skills all the time, and they will have to be flexible and adapt to these changes. It is very important to be ready for new opportunities.

Future Life

In the future, people will use robots at home. Robots will cook and clean. Cars will drive themselves, and we won’t need to drive. Students will study online and won’t go to school every day. We will use clean energy like wind and sun. People will live longer because medicine will be better. Some jobs will disappear, but life will be easier. I think the future will be exciting.
